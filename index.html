From Bean to Bar

What makes a great chocolate bar? We look at thousands of expert reviews and the countries where cocoa grows to find out.

1The verdict on 2,500 bars

One expert has tasted and rated 2,500 dark chocolate bars since 2006. How many of them are actually worth buying?

Each bar is scored from 1 to 5. Anything from 4 up is outstanding, and anything under 3 is disappointing.

The answer: only about 4 in every 100 bars reach outstanding, while about 1 in 5 is a disappointment.

2Where do the beans come from?

Cocoa only grows in a narrow belt near the equator. Do the countries that grow the most cocoa also grow the best?

The takeaway

Great beans often come from small growers. Papua New Guinea, Vietnam and Madagascar all beat the 3.20 average. Côte d'Ivoire grows about a third of the world's cocoa, yet its few reviewed bars average only 2.86.

Do the biggest growers make the best chocolate? The chart on the right compares how much cocoa each country grows with how well its bars are rated.

The countries growing the most cocoa are not the ones with the best-rated beans. Most of the world's cocoa goes into everyday chocolate, not craft bars.

3Who makes the bars?

Beans rarely become bars where they are grown. They travel across the world to craft makers, including several here in Australia.

Spotlight: Australia

Australian makers average 3.36, above the 3.20 average for all bars. They lean on our neighbours: 19 of their 48 single-origin bars use beans from Oceania, such as Papua New Guinea, Vanuatu and the Solomon Islands.

6Has chocolate got better over time?

Craft chocolate has boomed since 2006. Have the ratings risen with it? Mostly, yes: bars reviewed in 2006–2008 averaged 3.03, while bars from the most recent reviews average 3.32.

7Your chocolate aisle guide

Four things to look for next time you buy a bar.

Aim for 65–79% cocoa

Bars with 80% or more were disappointing almost 4 times in 10 and were almost never outstanding.

Keep it simple

Beans, sugar and cocoa butter scored best. Recipes with added vanilla scored lowest.

Choose a single origin

Blends of beans from several countries averaged 3.04, below the 3.21 for single-origin bars.

Try Pacific beans

Papua New Guinea, Vietnam and the Solomon Islands are among the best-rated origins, right on Australia's doorstep.
